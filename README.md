### Research Focus

I study **intervenable, planning-oriented physical world models and embodied intelligence**, alongside **causal discovery** and **reliable reasoning in foundation models**:

- **Embodied Intelligence & World Models** — Egocentric latent action learning ([EgoLAM](https://dzyy123.github.io/EgoLAM-Pages/)), causal structure plug-ins for counterfactual dynamics (*ECCV 2026*), trajectory-cost planning ([Traj-LeWM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14125)), and decoupled latent rollouts.
- **Visual & Statistical Causal Discovery** — Pixel-level active interventional discovery ([Intervene, Calibrate, Discover](https://dzyy123.github.io/intervene-calibrate-discover/)), iterative identification closure in linear SEMs, and Gaussianity-test-free LiNGAM ([GaussDetect-LiNGAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03428)).
- **Reliable LLM Causal Reasoning** — Probing representation–readout gaps (*EMNLP 2026 · Award Nomination*, [Causal Tongue-Tie](https://github.com/Dzyy123/tongue-tie-emnlp2026)) and calibrated multi-agent causal querying ([Tree-Query](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10137)).
